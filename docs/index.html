1

Listens

Joins the meeting as your stand-in and transcribes live.

2

Detects

Spots your name, your topics, or questions aimed at you.

3

Drafts

Looks up your docs, tickets and past meetings and drafts an answer.

4

Pings you

The question, the draft and its sources, on your phone.

5

You approve

Post as is, edit, join the call, or defer. Nothing is posted without you.

Transcribes Finnish & English

Live transcript with speaker names, including mixed-language discussion.

Pings only when it matters

Your name, your projects, direct questions. No noise from the rest.

Drafts from your own docs

Answers come from Confluence, Jira, SharePoint and past meetings, with the sources shown.

You approve every word

Nothing is posted without your tap. If it can't find an answer, it says so and just pings you.

Text only, never a voice

It never speaks in the meeting. Replies go to chat, clearly labelled as coming from your stand-in.

Summary afterwards

Decisions and your action items land in your inbox when the meeting ends.
